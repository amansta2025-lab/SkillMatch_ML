About the Attrition Prediction System

A machine-learning-based HR analytics tool that turns employee records into an early warning system for attrition.

Project Introduction

The Attrition Prediction System applies machine learning to the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset to analyze employee demographics, role information, compensation and satisfaction scores, and predict which employees are at risk of leaving.

Objectives

  • Analyze workforce demographics and role composition.
  • Study the relationship between satisfaction, overtime and attrition.
  • Predict individual employee attrition risk.
  • Surface the factors that drive employees to leave.
  • Support HR teams in planning retention interventions.

Dataset

The system is built on the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance dataset — 1,470 employee records across 35 attributes, including job role, department, income, satisfaction ratings, overtime status and tenure.

Technologies Used

  • Python
  • Flask
  • Pandas
  • NumPy
  • Scikit-learn
  • Matplotlib
  • HTML
  • CSS
